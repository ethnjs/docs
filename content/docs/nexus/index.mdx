## What NEXUS does

- **Volunteer profiles.** Contact details, experience, and availability live on the volunteer's account and carry forward to every tournament and season they join.
- **Volunteer assignment.** Directors manage events, shifts, and roles, and assign volunteers against real structural data instead of a spreadsheet.
- **Custom forms.** Tournaments and chapters build their own forms in NEXUS, with options pulled live from the tournament's data, and can edit them safely after responses come in.

Underneath all three is a membership and role model: a volunteer can belong to several tournaments and chapters, each with its own permissions, enforced by the system.
